APPLICATIONS

Entertainment

Video games are one of the most popular forms of entertainment today. People can play them for fun, watch videos about them, and even watch movies or television series based on them. In addition, most video games are highly accessible to the general public.

Education

Video games can contribute to education because they can serve as tools for cognitive development, stimulating logical reasoning, creativity, and problem-solving skills. They can also promote social skills, as many games encourage cooperation and respect for rules. Furthermore, games can increase engagement, as they can overcome resistance to difficult subjects and capture the audience's attention in a natural way. There are also many games specifically designed to teach and reinforce school-related content.

Interaction

Many video games offer multiplayer options, allowing players to interact with others from all around the world through online platforms or to play locally with several friends in the same place, for example. In this type of game, players can either cooperate with one another or compete against other people.
